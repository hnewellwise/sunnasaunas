      **Privacy Policy**

      Last updated: 4/9/25

      We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under GDPR.

      ### What We Collect

      When you use our website, we may collect:

      - Basic usage data (pages visited, time on site, clicks, etc.) through Google Analytics.
      - Information you choose to share with us (for example, if you contact us directly by email or form).

      ### How We Use Your Data

      We use this data to:

      - Understand how people use our website and make improvements.
      - Respond to enquiries or requests you send us.

      ### Sharing with Third Parties

      - The only third party we share data with is Google Analytics, which helps us understand website traffic.
      - All other data stays in-house and is handled in line with GDPR. We never sell or rent your personal data.

      ### Cookies

      Google Analytics uses cookies to track anonymous usage data. You can opt out at any time by adjusting your browser settings or using Google’s opt-out tools.

      ### Your Rights

      Under GDPR, you have the right to:

      - Access the personal data we hold about you.
      - Request corrections or deletion of your data.
      - Object to certain types of processing.
      - Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

      To exercise these rights, please contact us.

      ### Data Storage & Security

      We store all personal data securely and only for as long as necessary.

      ### Contact Us

      If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us.
